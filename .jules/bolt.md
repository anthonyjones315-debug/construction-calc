## 2025-05-14 - Avoiding Scope Creep in Performance Tasks
**Learning:** Even when a pre-existing bug or test failure is discovered (like a missing material list in a template), fixing it during a performance-focused task can lead to a "Mostly Correct" review and is considered scope creep.
**Action:** Strictly limit changes to performance optimizations. Document pre-existing issues but do not attempt to fix them unless they are blockers for the optimization itself.
