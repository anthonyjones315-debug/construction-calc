
## 2026-04-16 - Self-Contained Interaction Feedback
**Learning:** Adding local state feedback (like "Copied" text and emerald styling) directly to the trigger element provides much stronger confirmation than distant toast messages, especially in tool-heavy interfaces where the user's focus is tight on the result area.
**Action:** Use transient state transitions on action buttons to provide immediate "interaction successful" signals before/instead of global notifications.
